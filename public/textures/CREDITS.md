- **earth-night** — NASA / NOAA / DMSP — Earth’s City Lights (2000). Domaine public.
  Source : https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/55000/55167/earth_lights_lrg.jpg
- **earth-clouds** — NASA Earth Observatory — Blue Marble : couverture nuageuse (MODIS). Domaine public.
  Source : https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_2048.jpg
- **earth-spec** — masque océans dérivé automatiquement de `earth-day`.
